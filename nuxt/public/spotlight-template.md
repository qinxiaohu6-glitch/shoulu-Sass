- Write for a reader who found the page from a search and has never heard of the product.

## Before you send — self-check

- [ ] Original text, or a substantial rewrite. The homepage copy is not an article.
- [ ] 800–1,500 words in total.
- [ ] Every section has real content — no "TBD", no heading with one sentence under it.
- [ ] Contains both the product URL and the listing URL.
- [ ] No invented quotes, user counts, revenue figures or rankings.
- [ ] No superlatives that cannot be checked ("the best", "the only", "#1").
- [ ] No medical, financial or legal claims.
- [ ] Every link points at a page that loads.

---

## How the links will be handled

| Link | Where it appears | rel |
|---|---|---|
| Product website | The product card at the top of the article | `dofollow` |
| Listing page | Card, call to action, breadcrumb | internal |
| Any other external link | Inside the article body | `nofollow noopener` |

Links in the body also carry `utm_source=product-atlas` so you can see the
traffic in your own analytics.

## What happens after you send it

1. You get a yes, or a specific list of changes, within five working days.
2. A no comes with the reason, so a second draft is not a guess.
3. On approval the article is edited for typos and padding only — no claims added,
   no claims softened.
4. It goes live at `/spotlights/<slug>` and into the sitemap the same day.
